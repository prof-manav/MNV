Brief bio

Muktinath Vishwakarma is an AI researcher and Ph.D. scholar in Computer Science and Engineering at VNIT Nagpur, India. The research centres on multi-step forecasting: multi-scale fusion Transformers, Fourier–LSTM hybrids with direct multi-output heads, and Ornstein–Uhlenbeck mean-reversion priors that curb drift at long horizons, benchmarked on aluminium and multi-metal price panels.

Other interests include Temporal Fusion Transformers, Kolmogorov–Arnold networks, graph neural networks, anomaly detection, and retrieval-augmented generation for legal documents.

Core Research Themes

  1. Multi-Scale and Frequency-Aware Forecasting

    Fusing frequency-domain priors with hierarchical temporal encoders and direct multi-output heads, so that long-horizon forecasts accumulate less error. This covers MSF-Transformer and FANformer.

  2. Physics-Informed Constraints

    Injecting Ornstein–Uhlenbeck mean-reversion priors into deep sequence models to regularise long-horizon drift in commodity price series (details).

  3. Anomaly Detection in Time Series

    Organising statistical, machine-learning, and Transformer-based detectors by setting: univariate or multivariate, streaming or batch, reconstruction or prediction (survey).
