Brief bio

Muktinath Vishwakarma is a Ph.D. scholar in Computer Science and Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, supervised by Prof. Manish P. Kurhekar. The research focuses on generative artificial intelligence and its applications in time series data analysis, with papers on graph neural networks for multi-step forecasting, agricultural and commodity price forecasting, expected-shortfall forecasting, reinforcement-learning trading, and interpretable models for concrete and slope-stability data.

Muktinath holds an M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Kanpur and a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University, and qualified UGC JRF and NET in 2016. Before joining VNIT, Muktinath was a Teaching Assistant Professor at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur, and Director of The Altius Classes, Nagpur, bringing more than eight years of teaching experience.

Core Research Themes

  1. Multi-Step and Long-Term Forecasting

    Graph neural networks built around a target series (ResNode-GCN, target-indexed residual GNNs), low-rank calendar priors for parameter-efficient long-term forecasting, and feature selection that depends on both lookback and horizon.

  2. Agricultural and Commodity Prices

    Multi-market potato price forecasting: a structure-aware wavelet-curvature loss, Kolmogorov–Arnold networks with geographic priors, and a study of how forecastable weekly prices are relative to persistence.

  3. Financial Risk and Trading

    Reinforcement-learning day trading with attention-LSTM, CNN, and explainable state modelling, and expected-shortfall forecasting with local tail adaptation.

  4. Interpretable Machine Learning for Engineering

    Hydration-curve neural additive models for concrete strength and physics-guided, cost-sensitive XGBoost for slope-stability classification.
